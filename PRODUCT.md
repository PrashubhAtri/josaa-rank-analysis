# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students, parents, and counselors use this tool while evaluating JEE counselling options. They are usually comparing many institute, program, quota, category, gender pool, and round combinations under time pressure, and need a fast way to understand what a historical JoSAA rank could have reached.

## Product Purpose

JoSAA Rank Analysis turns historical JoSAA opening and closing rank data into a practical filtering and comparison interface. A user enters a rank, selects year and round context, narrows the relevant seat combinations, and sees which options were historically possible, not possible, or missing data.

Success means the user can quickly build a realistic shortlist, compare historical movement across years, export the visible results, and understand that the output is a reference based on past cutoffs rather than a prediction or official counselling result.

## Brand Personality

Fast, clear, reassuring.

The interface should feel direct and trustworthy: quick to scan, careful about caveats, and calm enough for high-stakes academic decision-making.

## Anti-references

Avoid a marketing-site treatment, decorative academic imagery, vague admissions-coaching language, and visuals that imply prediction certainty. Do not make the product feel like an official JoSAA portal clone, a coaching-center lead form, or a generic analytics dashboard detached from the student's decision.

Avoid relying on color alone for status meaning. Possible, not possible, and no-data states need text labels, shape, or other redundant cues.

## Design Principles

Historical, not predictive: the UI must keep the distinction visible without slowing expert users down.

Rank-first workflow: filters, presets, summaries, and exports should serve the user's current rank analysis instead of becoming separate browsing modes.

Dense but legible: the product earns compact tables and controls, but labels, focus states, and mobile layouts must stay readable.

Reassurance through precision: use concrete labels, clear units, exact year and round context, and explicit data-missing states.

Fast comparison: make common comparisons, such as IITs, top fields, years, and visible-result export, accessible without manual repetition.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for contrast, focus visibility, labels, keyboard navigation, and responsive behavior. Support reduced motion for any animations. Status colors must have redundant textual indicators so color-blind users can still understand result meaning.
